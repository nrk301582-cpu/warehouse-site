Our main service

Bonded warehouse

Keep imported goods in a customs-bonded warehouse and clear them in parts as your buyers need them, instead of paying duty on everything at once.

Each cargo has one record. Search it by SAC no or Bill of Entry no and the same data opens: goods received, goods taken out, goods left, last movement, weight and 20 ft / 40 ft containers.

Ask about bonded storage

  1. Goods arriveWe record the SAC no, Bill of Entry no, weight and containers.
  2. Stored under bondYour cargo stays in the bonded area until you clear it.
  3. Cleared in partsEvery removal is logged with date and quantity.
  4. You track it onlineType your SAC no or Bill of Entry no to see your balance.
