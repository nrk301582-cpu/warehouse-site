Our core business

Bonded warehousing for imported goods

A bonded warehouse is a warehouse where imported goods can be stored under customs supervision. OWPL provides bonded storage for imported goods at Ramgarh, Chandigarh Road, Ludhiana.

Each cargo has one record, identified by its SAC no and Bill of Entry no. Our staff enter the details, and you can check your goods online at any time on the Track cargo page.

Customs formalities, duty treatment and any permissions depend on customs rules and on the approvals that apply to your goods. Your C.H.A. and our team can advise you on your case.

Knowledge for importers

Import guide

Simple notes for anyone importing goods into India. This is general information. Customs rules and duty rates change, so always confirm with your C.H.A. or customs before you act.

1. Before you import

Get an Importer Exporter Code (IEC). Check that your goods are not restricted or prohibited. Find the correct HS code, because it decides the duty rate and any licence or certificate you need.

2. Key documents

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, your IEC, and the Bill of Entry. Some goods also need certificates such as origin, quality or licence papers. Names and quantities must match on every document.

3. Who is a C.H.A.?

A Customs House Agent (also called a customs broker) is licensed to file your customs papers, pay duty and deal with customs officers for you.

4. Normal clearance

Goods arrive, your C.H.A. files the Bill of Entry online, customs assesses it, you pay duty, goods may be examined, and then customs allows delivery.

5. Clearance through bond

An into-bond Bill of Entry is used when imported goods are to be stored in a bonded warehouse. An ex-bond Bill of Entry is used when they are taken out. How duty applies depends on customs rules, and your C.H.A. will advise you.

6. Duties you may pay

Basic customs duty, a social welfare surcharge, and IGST. Some goods have extra duties. The amount depends on the goods, their value and the HS code.

Tips to save money and time

Check the HS code early. A wrong code can mean the wrong duty and delays.

Do not leave containers waiting. Port and shipping line charges (demurrage and detention) grow every day.

Plan transport before arrival. Book the truck and the warehouse space in advance.

Decide storage early. Know where your goods will be stored before they arrive.
